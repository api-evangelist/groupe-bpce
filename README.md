Groupe BPCE is the second-largest banking group in France, operating retail banking and insurance through its main brands Banque Populaire, Caisse d'Epargne and Natixis (per groupebpce.com). Through its Group BPCE API STORE (apistore.groupebpce.com) it publishes an API product catalog and developer center covering payment and collection services and PSD2 (DSP2) regulatory APIs for account information, payment initiation and funds confirmation for authorized payment service providers, with technical and sales support. It also publishes its API design guidelines and Spectral rulesets on GitHub (github.com/BPCE).

**Areas:** Company · Banking · Financial Services · Open Banking · PSD2 · Payments · Insurance · France
